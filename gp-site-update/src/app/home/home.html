G Pandey Trust (Dr. Gangeshwar Pandey Trust) works on food camps and ration distribution, educational camps and medical camps. Food, medicines and other donations are always welcome — they go straight to the people we serve.

Jeevan Jayate

Our non-profit organization deals with food rescue and other social activities. It was initiated by Mr. Binod Kumar Pandey to bridge the gap between hunger and excess food. The organization's objective is to help people during a pandemic or any natural calamity.

We aim to eliminate hunger, malnutrition and food wastage, many meals at a time, along with flood relief, Covid support, fulfilling medicine shortages and more.

And we are always here to help, with passion and innovation, for the welfare and upliftment of people.
